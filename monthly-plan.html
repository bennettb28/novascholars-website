Monthly Plan Questions

How does recurring billing work?

Your card is charged $175 once a month on your enrollment date. You can cancel anytime before the next billing cycle by emailing novascholarsacademy@icloud.com.

Do I have to book all 4 sessions at once?

No—you can schedule all four weekly slots immediately after enrolling, or return to the booking calendar anytime during your active month.

No problem! You can book a one-time Single Tutoring Session ($50) instead without enrolling in a monthly plan.
