About NovaScholars Academy

Building deep understanding through relatable, peer-to-peer mentorship.

Our Mission

"NovaScholars Academy was founded to help students build deep understanding, not just complete assignments."

Our mission is to provide personalized academic support that strengthens critical thinking, confidence, and long-term success.

Why NovaScholars Exists

Many students struggle not because they lack ability, but because they haven’t been given the time or structure to truly understand the material. NovaScholars was created to close that gap through focused, individualized instruction.

Our Founder & Philosophy

NovaScholars was founded by Brendan Bennett, a student deeply engaged in STEM education, research, and academic mentorship. With experience across rigorous coursework, the goal is to help other students navigate challenging material with clarity and confidence.

Teaching Philosophy: Sessions focus on reasoning, patterns, and connections rather than memorization. Each lesson is structured around the student’s current level, goals, and learning style.

Who We Work With

We work with middle and high school students seeking academic support in STEM subjects, enrichment, or skill-building. Ideal for students who want clarity, challenge, and meaningful progress.
