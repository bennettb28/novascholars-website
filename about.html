About NovaScholars Academy

Building deep understanding through relatable, individualized peer-to-peer mentorship.

Our Mission

“NovaScholars Academy was founded to help students build deep understanding, not just complete assignments.”

Our mission is to provide personalized academic support that strengthens critical thinking, study confidence, and long-term independence—whether through live 1-on-1 tutoring or thoughtfully organized study resources.

Why NovaScholars Exists

Many students struggle not because they lack ability, but because they haven’t been given the time, structure, or clear explanations needed to truly grasp the material. NovaScholars was created to close that gap through focused, individualized instruction tailored to how each student learns best.

Our Founder & Philosophy

NovaScholars was founded by Brendan Bennett, a student deeply engaged in STEM education, research, and academic mentorship. Having navigated rigorous coursework firsthand, Brendan started NovaScholars to help other students approach challenging subjects with clarity and confidence.

Teaching Philosophy: Sessions focus on reasoning, patterns, and connections rather than rote memorization. Every lesson and study resource is adapted to the student’s current level, class expectations, and personal learning style.

Who We Work With

We work with middle and high school students seeking supportive academic guidance in STEM subjects, core coursework, and organization or study skills—alongside parents who want dependable, transparent communication and structured support.
