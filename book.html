Tutoring FAQs

How do I avoid paying twice?

Complete your $50 Stripe payment once—Stripe automatically sends you to our confirmation page with the Calendly calendar. If you have already paid and need to reschedule or book later, use the calendar above without clicking Pay again.

What if I need to reschedule?

You can reschedule through your Calendly confirmation email up to 12 hours before your session starts.

What platform do sessions use?

Sessions are held online via Google Meet or Zoom with screen sharing and digital whiteboarding.
